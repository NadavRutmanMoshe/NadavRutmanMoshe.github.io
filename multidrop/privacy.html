Privacy Policy for Multidrop

Last updated: 24 November 2026

Multidrop ("the game") is a casual game published by Nadav Rutman Moshe, trading as Nadaz Games ("we", "us"). This policy explains what the game does with information, in plain language. It applies to the Android and iOS versions of the game.

The short version. The game itself collects nothing about you and sends nothing to us. Your progress is saved on your own device. The only other company involved is Google AdMob, and only for the optional video ads you choose to watch.

1. What the game stores on your device

The game saves your progress on your device so it can remember where you were: levels completed, stars, coins, the skins you own and use, and your settings (such as sound on or off). We never receive this information. If your phone backs itself up (Google backup on Android, iCloud on iPhone), that backup can include it, so your progress can return when you reinstall the game. That backup is kept in your own Google or Apple account and we cannot see it. Uninstalling the game deletes it from the device.

2. Advertisements

The game is free and shows optional rewarded video ads: you choose to watch one in return for an in-game reward (for example extra coins, extra balls or a hint). There are no ads you have to watch. The ads are provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising identifier (Android Advertising ID, or Apple's IDFA only if you allow tracking)
  • your IP address and the approximate location derived from it
  • device and app information, such as the device model, operating system, language and app version
  • ad interactions (ads shown, tapped and completed) and diagnostic data

How Google uses this data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

3. Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The game itself collects no personal information from anyone.

4. Consent in Europe

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the game asks for your consent through Google's consent tool before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time from the Levels screen → Privacy settings.

5. Your choices

  • iPhone: iOS asks whether the game may track you. If you decline, ads are not personalised with your IDFA. You can change this in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Android: you can reset or delete your advertising ID in Settings → Privacy → Ads.
  • You never have to watch an ad: every ad in the game is optional.

6. Security, and how long anything is kept

We do not keep any data about you, because we do not receive any. Data processed by Google AdMob is kept according to Google's own policies linked above.

7. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.
